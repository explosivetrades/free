Prop firm chunne se pehle

Sirf sasta challenge dekh kar firm mat chuniye. Ye cheezein pehle check karein.

✓ Ye check karein

  • Daily loss floating loss ke saath ginta hai ya sirf closed trades par
  • Max loss static hai ya trailing
  • Swing trading mein weekend aur overnight trade hold karne ki permission
  • News trading rules aur consistency rule
  • Payout kitne din mein aur kis tareeke se milta hai
  • Aapka platform (MT5 ya cTrader) available hai ya nahi

✕ In cheezon se bachein

  • "Instant funding" jaise plans jinke rules itne tight hon ki pass hona lagbhag mushkil ho
  • Bina padhe challenge kharidna. Har firm ki Terms of Service padhein.
  • VPN ya VPS ka galat use. Kai firms isse account band kar deti hain.
  • Ek hi trade mein poora target lene ki koshish. Daily loss limit sabse jaldi wahi todta hai.
