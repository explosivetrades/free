Trend ke rules

Zones practice karne se pehle 4H aur 1H chart par Supertrend (10-7) dekhein.

4H + 1H Green

Up Trend

4H aur 1H dono mein Supertrend green hai to trend up hai. Zones ko up trend ke rules ke hisaab se practice karein.

4H + 1H Sell

Down Trend

4H aur 1H dono mein Supertrend sell hai to trend down hai. Zones ko down trend ke rules ke hisaab se practice karein.

Dhyan rakhein: 4H aur 1H alag-alag direction mein hon to trend clear nahi hai. Dono same hone ka wait karein.

Zone strategy ka use kaise karein

Har din ka simple routine.

  1. Subah 8:45 AM ke baad chart kholkar din ke zones dekhein.
  2. High zone, Low zone aur main support-resistance area note karein.
  3. 4H aur 1H par Supertrend (10-7) se trend confirm karein.
  4. Trend ke hisaab se sirf rules wale zones practice karein.
